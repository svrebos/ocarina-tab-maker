How to read the tab
    Saving your tabs
    • Nothing is stored on a server. Instead, the address of this page contains your whole tab: the notes, title, subtitle, line width and ocarina type.
    • Press Show tab, then bookmark the page (Ctrl+D, or Cmd+D on a Mac). The bookmark is named after your title.
    • Tip: keep all your tabs together in one bookmark folder, for example “Ocarina tabs”. That is your collection, and it syncs with your browser account.
    • The Copy share link button gives you the same address, handy for sending a tab to someone else.
    • Changed something? Press Show tab again before bookmarking, so the address is up to date.
